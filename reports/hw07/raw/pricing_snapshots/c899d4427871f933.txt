Capital Bikeshare For All
Equity on two wheels
We’re working to expand access to bikeshare. It’s part of our ongoing effort to help create safer and healthier communities, and celebrate the diversity that makes our region so incredible.
Download the app
Reducing fares with
Capital Bikeshare for All
Capital Bikeshare is proud to offer $5 annual memberships for qualifying residents.
Join for $5/year
Same member benefits. Only $5/year.
Members get unlimited 60-minute rides on classic bikes — and a whole lot more. Capital Bikeshare for All members get a $40 monthly ride credit each month to cover some of the costs of using Capital Bikeshare, including:
Per-minute ebike charges
Per-minute classic bike charges for rides longer than 60 minutes
Out-of-station parking charges for ebikes
The $40 monthly ride credit renews each month, however, unused ride credit does not roll over to the next month. The monthly ride credit doesn’t cover fees for parking violations — these fees are always the responsibility of the rider.
If you want to park your Capital Bikeshare ebike at any public bike rack within the service area, you’ll pay $2 each time. Remember, this is covered by the $40 monthly ride credit until the credit is used up, and you can always avoid the $2 charge by docking at a CaBi station.
Who’s eligible
Capital Bikeshare for All is available to residents of Washington, DC; Arlington, VA; Alexandria, VA; Prince George's County, MD; Fairfax County, VA; City of Falls Church, VA; City of Fairfax, VA; and Montgomery County, MD who qualify for a state or federal assistance program including, Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP); Women, Infants, and Children (WIC); Temporary Assistance to Needy Families (TANF); SSI/SSDI Supplemental Security Income; Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP); Veterans rated with 60% disability or more, and Medicaid.
We accept the following forms for program qualification:
EBT Card
Discounted utility bill dated in last 30 days
SSI/SSDI Statement of benefits
Medicaid Card (with date of birth)*
VA Letter
*If your Medicaid card does not include your date of birth, there are other secondary forms of identification you can use to verify age including: a valid state ID with picture, a US passport, consular card, a US birth certificate/card, valid military or military dependent ID card.
Benefits
Unlimited, free 60-minute rides on classic bikes. CaBi for All members pay $0.05 per minute, after the included 60 minutes.
A discounted rate on ebikes. CaBi for All members pay $0.10 per minute.
A discount on out-of-station ebike parking. Members pay $2 each time.
A $40 monthly ride credit to cover ride time for per-minute ebike rates, per-minute rates for classic bike rides longer than 60 minutes, and out-of-station parking charges for ebikes.
Pay just $5/year.
Requirements
Members must be 16 or older.
Proof that you receive SNAP, WIC, TANF, SSI/SSDI, LIHEAP, Veterans disability (60%+), or Medicaid assistance.
How to get started
The easiest way to get going is to use the app, but we can provide bike keys to members who may not have smartphones.
And, for those who don’t have a debit, credit card, or prepaid card required for a standard Capital Bikeshare membership, the program incorporates a cash payment system at select locations. Capital Bikeshare requires all riders to keep an active credit or debit card on file in case any extra time charges are incurred — for example, if you keep a bike out for longer than the time allowed in your membership or if you lose your bike.

Additional pricing
The first 60 minutes of each ride on a classic bike is included in the membership. Unlocks are always free for both classic bikes and ebikes.
Members pay $0.10 per minute for ebikes (with no included ride time) and $0.05 per minute for classic bikes for any ride longer than 60 minutes. Parking an ebike outside of a CaBi station costs $2.
Renewing your membership
Participants can opt out at any time. Members receive a reminder email 7 days before their annual payment is due so they can adjust their membership if needed.

DAY PASSES
$10/day
24 hours of Capital Bikeshare Access
Unlimited 45-minute rides on a classic bikes for 24 hours.
Perfect for tourists and visitors
Explore DC like never before – on two wheels!
A Day Pass is $10 and includes unlimited 45-minute rides on classic bikes. Ride longer than 45 minutes and pay $0.05/minute. Day Pass members enjoy no unlock fees and a discounted rate of $0.15/min on ebikes.
Capital Bikeshare is a fun way to see the sights and get to local attractions. Take a bike out for a ride around the National Mall or explore the trails and scenery along the Potomac River.
How to Buy a Pass
Day Passes can be purchased in the Capital Bikeshare mobile app or from a kiosk .
Additional Pricing
Ebike parking is free at Capital Bikeshare stations. Day pass holders will be charged $2 for parking an ebike at a public bike rack in the service area. A $25 fee will be applied to any ebike that is not docked at a station or secured to a public bike rack with its cable lock, if it remains inactive for over 30 minutes after the ride concludes. This fee is in addition to the standard per-minute rate.
If you upgrade to an ebike or incur any extra time fees, your card on file will be charged. The fee for a lost or stolen bike is $1,200.

30-Day Membership
In town for more than a few days? Enjoy the convenience of a Capital Bikeshare 30-Day Membership.
Capital Bikeshare is a fun and affordable way to get around the metro DC area and a great alternative to the train, taxis, buses or walking. It’s designed for quick trips with convenience in mind. Use it to commute to work or school, run errands, get to appointments and more.
The 30-Day Membership comes with a bike key that lets you skip the kiosk and get access to thousands of bikes in Washington, DC; Arlington, VA; Alexandria, VA; Montgomery County, MD; Prince George's County, MD; Fairfax County, VA; the City of Fairfax; and the City of Falls Church, VA.
You can take as many 45-minute trips on classic bikes as you want while your membership is active. Ride longer than 45 minutes and pay $0.05/minute. A 30-Day membership means you don't pay to unlock bikes and enjoy a discounted rate of $0.15/min on ebikes.
Additional pricing
Ebike parking is free at Capital Bikeshare stations. As a 30-day Membership holder, you'll be charged $2 for parking an ebike at a public bike rack in the service area. A $25 fee will be applied to any ebike that is not docked at a station or secured to a public bike rack with its cable lock, if it remains inactive for over 30 minutes after the ride concludes. This fee is in addition to the standard per-minute rate.
If you upgrade to an ebike or incur any extra time fees, your card on file will be charged. The fee for a lost or stolen bike is $1,200.
